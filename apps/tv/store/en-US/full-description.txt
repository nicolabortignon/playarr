Playarr plays the music on your own server on your TV: the albums you own, kept on your computer or NAS, from the sofa with the remote.

Playarr is a free, open-source music server that you run yourself, at home or on a machine you rent. It reads the music folders you give it and, if you use Lidarr, the library Lidarr manages. This app is its player for Android TV. You need a Playarr server to use it: set one up from github.com/nicolabortignon/playarr, or ask someone who runs one for an account.

SIGN IN FROM THE SOFA
• The TV finds Playarr servers on your home network, or you type the address with the remote
• No password to type: the TV shows a code, and you approve it on your phone by scanning its QR code
• One server, many people: each has their own account, history, favorites and playlists, and the TV switches between them

LISTEN
• Gapless albums
• Even volume: every song at the same loudness, prepared by your server
• DJ mix: songs blend into each other on the beat, mixed by your server
• Synced lyrics, a sleep timer, and radio that keeps playing similar songs from your library when the queue ends
• The music plays on behind the screensaver and other apps, with the TV's own media controls

TEN FEET AWAY
• Home, with Jump back in, mixes made from what you listen to, and Feeling lucky
• Search, artists, albums, favorites and playlists, all with the remote
• Now Playing with the cover large, and an art mode that keeps an OLED screen safe when it's left up
• On a Philips TV, the Ambilight can take the colors of the cover playing (off unless you turn it on)
• Where the server's owner allows it, discover music and ask for an album to be added to the server's Lidarr library

NOTHING ELSE
Playarr talks only to the servers you add (and, for the Ambilight, to the TV itself). No ads, no tracking, no account with us.
