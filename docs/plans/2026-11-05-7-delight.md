# Plan 7: Small delights

Proposed 2026-11-05 from the review of every screen described in [plan 5](2026-11-05-5-ui-consistency.md#how-the-screens-were-reviewed); not agreed yet. Written with [plan 5, UI consistency](2026-11-05-5-ui-consistency.md) and [plan 6, UX](2026-11-05-6-ux-flows.md). Plans 5 and 6 come first: delight on top of inconsistency reads as noise.

**Goal:** the moments around the music feel made by hand.
- Things move from where they came from.
- The app keeps time with the song it's playing, which only Playarr can do, because it knows every song's beat grid.
- The words have one voice: the record-shop voice already in a few places runs through every corner.

Each item is small, is the same idea on both apps, and stays still under Reduce Motion (Settings → Appearance → Motion, or the system's setting).

## What's already lovely, and the bar for new ideas

- Feeling lucky's sparkles, which twinkle while it picks.
- The playlist covers drawn from the songs, and the picker with six named styles.
- Synced lyrics you can tap to jump to a line, on both apps.
- The 20-second fade at the end of a sleep timer (`sleep.ts`, `SleepTimer.swift`).
- The accent that follows the album art.
- The heart that pops when you favorite a song (iOS `.symbolEffect(.bounce)`, web `animate-pop`).
- Rediscover's dice.
- The 404: "Side C. This groove is empty."

New delights should feel like these: quiet, on-theme, and gone if you don't look for them.

## 1. Things move from where they came from — S–M

- **Covers that grow:** tapping a cover grows it into the album page's header, and the mini player's cover grows into Now Playing's. Going back shrinks it home.
  - iOS: `matchedTransitionSource` with `.navigationTransition(.zoom)`. Neither app has a shared-element transition today.
  - Web: the View Transitions API, with a `view-transition-name` on the cover.
- **Play and Pause morph** into each other rather than swapping: SF Symbols' `.contentTransition(.symbolEffect(.replace))` on iOS, a path morph on the web.
- **The accent glides** to the next song's color over about a beat. The web already transitions `--accent` over 0.8 s; on iOS, animate `theme.accent` the same way.
- **Done when** opening an album from a cover and closing Now Playing feel like one object moving, on both apps, and nothing moves under Reduce Motion.

## 2. The app keeps time with the music — M

Every analyzed song has a beat grid and bars. No other player at home can use that.

- **Now-playing bars:** the bars on the current song's row and in the mini player follow the music's actual level and land on the beat, instead of looping.
  - Web: an `AnalyserNode` on the Web Audio graph that desktop browsers already use.
  - iOS: a tap on the engine's mixer.
  - Songs without a beat grid keep today's loop.
- **A breathing glow:** in Now Playing, a soft glow behind the cover breathes on each bar's downbeat. It stays barely visible, never flashes, and is off under Reduce Motion.
- **DJ blends you can see:** during a DJ transition, the mini player shows both covers overlapping, cross-fading as the songs do, and Now Playing says "Mixing into Teardrop" for the length of the blend. This builds on `TransitionView`, which already knows the next transition.
- **Done when** a DJ mix of two analyzed songs shows the blend in the mini player and Now Playing, and the bars stop when the music does.

## 3. Covers that feel like objects — S

- **Color while loading:** a loading cover's space shows the album's own color instead of gray. The web picks an accent from the art in the browser (`lib/color.ts`); pick it once on the server and send it with each album, so every placeholder and iOS can use it too.
- **Pause and play:** pausing lets the Now Playing cover settle back slightly (smaller, shadow softer), and playing lifts it again. You can tell the state from across the room.
- **Tilt:** in Now Playing, the cover tilts a degree or two with the phone, or with the pointer on a desktop, catching a faint sheen. It stays still under Reduce Motion.
- **A living backdrop:** Now Playing's backdrop drifts slowly through the cover's colors instead of sitting still.
- **Done when** no cover ever shows as a gray square, and Now Playing tells paused from playing without reading a button.
- *Server done 2026-11-06: each album's `coverColor` and `coverAccent`, read once (Lidarr's covers, folder pictures, and the Cover Art Archive fallbacks) and sent with every album and song.*
- *Web done 2026-11-06: placeholders take `coverColor`, the accent `coverAccent` at once; Now Playing's cover settles back when paused, tilts toward a mouse with a sheen, and its backdrop drifts through the cover's colors. iOS to do.*

## 4. One voice, everywhere — S

The record-shop voice is in a few places: Rediscover's "Pulled from the crates", Home's kickers, "Side C". Give every empty and error state a line in that voice, from one list in `docs/design.md` (plan 5 §7), each with the action that fills the page (plan 5 §8). For example:

| Where | Today | In the voice |
|---|---|---|
| Queue, empty | "Nothing queued" | "The deck's empty." + Feeling lucky, Jump back in |
| Downloads, empty | "Nothing downloaded yet." | "Nothing in the record bag yet." + how to download |
| Favorites, empty | "Nothing hearted yet." | "No hearts yet." + Jump back in |
| Search, no results | "Not in your crates." | keep, + what to do next (plan 6 §7) |
| An error | "Something skipped." | "The needle skipped." + Try again |

- **Home greets by name and time:** "Morning, Alex", "Late night, Alex", using the hours Vibes of the hour already uses. Both apps greet (plan 5 §4).
- **Done when** every empty and error state on both apps uses a line from the list.
- *The list is written (2026-11-06): `docs/design.md`, Empty, loading and error → The lines. The web's empty states already match it except the queue, Favorites, Downloads, Stats and a failed page; iOS takes it with plan 5 §8.*
- *Web done 2026-11-06: every empty and failed page says its line; Home greets by the time of day and name ("Afternoon, Alex."). iOS has the lines (plan 5 §8); its greeting to do.*

## 5. Little rituals — S

- **Feeling lucky:** while it picks, three or four covers flick past like a hand flipping through a crate, landing on the first song. The sparkles already show it's working; this shows what it found.
- **The end of a song:** ten seconds before it ends, the mini player's subtitle turns to "Next: Teardrop", or "Mixing into Teardrop on the next bar" with DJ mix.
- **A finished download:** the ring closes into its tick (as now), then comes a light haptic and a toast: "Mezzanine is on this iPhone".
- **Sleep timer:** when it ends, Now Playing dims to a moon and "Sleep well" for a moment before the screen rests.
- **Stats:**
  - the headline number counts up when the page opens;
  - a new longest streak gets a small flag;
  - the year in review opens like a sleeve sliding out.
- **Reordering:** dragging a song lifts it (shadow, slight scale) and the rest part smoothly. A light tick per slot on iOS already exists for the queue (`QueueView.swift:56`); playlists should get it too.
- **A `?` overlay on desktop** lists the keyboard shortcuts as a sleeve's track list. Today they live only in Settings → Playback.
- **Done when** each ritual is in both apps, behind Reduce Motion where it moves.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: Feeling lucky's flick, the end-of-song line (with the DJ wording), the download toast, Sleep well, Stats' count-up, New record flag and sleeve, and the lift when reordering (docs/design.md, Moments). iOS to do.*

## 6. Scrubbing you can see — S

- **Energy under the seek bar:** the energy strip (`EnergyStrip.tsx`) appears today only in Stats for nerds, the transition view and Mix points. Put it under Now Playing's seek bar on the web, and draw the same picture on iOS, so you can see the drop and the quiet bridge before you jump.
- **Detents on iOS:** scrubbing gets a light detent haptic at each section boundary (intro, drop, outro, from the mix map).
- **Done when** an analyzed song's seek bar shows its shape on both apps.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: the energy strip under Now Playing's seek bar, seeking where pressed, its room reserved for songs without one. iOS to do (and its detents).*

## 7. Haptics, on purpose — S

- **One vocabulary**, all through `Haptic`:
  - toggle: favorite, shuffle, repeat;
  - success: download done, request sent, Feeling lucky landed;
  - selection: scrubbing detents, reorder ticks;
  - warning: a removal, alongside its Undo toast.
- **Today** some taps go through `Haptic`, others are written inline (`FavoriteButton.swift:30`, `AlbumView.swift:134`, `HomeView.swift:85`). The favorite button is one of them: it uses `.impact(weight: .light)`, though `Haptic.toggle` is documented for favorites. Downloads, playlist reordering and Add to playlist have no haptic at all.
- **Done when** every haptic in the app comes from `Haptic`, and the four kinds above cover them.

## Bigger ideas, for later

These are larger than small delights; each would need its own plan.
- **iOS extensions** (the app has none today):
  - a Home Screen widget (Jump back in, Feeling lucky);
  - a Control Center control for Feeling lucky;
  - a Live Activity for the sleep timer and DJ mixes.
- **A share card:** "Now playing" as an image with the cover, the accent and the song, through ShareLink on iOS and Web Share on the web.
- **Word-by-word lyrics** when the LRC file has word timings (enhanced LRC).
- **A vinyl Now Playing**, held sideways: the cover slides aside to show a record spinning, its label the cover, which stops when paused. iOS already draws a vinyl for artists without a picture.
- **"Take me from A to B":** a radio that travels from one song to another through songs that blend, using the ordering that mixes already use (`packages/mix/src/blend.ts`).

## Order

5 → 4 → 1 → 7 → 3 → 6 → 2. The rituals and the voice are the cheapest and the most felt. Keeping time with the music (2) is the signature, but it needs analyzed songs and both audio engines, so it goes last.
