# Plan 6: Flows that work everywhere

Proposed 2026-11-05 from the review of every screen described in [plan 5](2026-11-05-5-ui-consistency.md#how-the-screens-were-reviewed); agreed 2026-11-06 and being built (where it stands, below). Written with [plan 5, UI consistency](2026-11-05-5-ui-consistency.md) and [plan 7, delight](2026-11-05-7-delight.md). Where an item is already in another plan, this one points to it rather than repeating it.

**Goal:** everything you can do takes the fewest steps and works with a finger, a mouse, a keyboard and a screen reader, on a phone, a tablet and a desktop. Nothing leaves you stuck, and nothing is lost without a way back.

## 1. Touch at desktop width — S — done 2026-11-06

*Done: song rows' heart and ⋯, the playlist's and queue's drag handles and menus, and Requests' remove hide until hover only with a mouse (`pointer-fine:`); the cards' quick-play buttons are hidden on touch screens (`pointer-coarse:hidden`) rather than below `md`, so an iPad no longer has an invisible Play over each cover's corner.*

**The problem:** on an iPad or a touch laptop, the web app hides song rows' heart, ⋯ menu and drag handles until you hover, and hover never comes. They're hidden by screen width, not by whether the device can hover:
- `md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100` in `TrackList.tsx:129`, `FavoriteButton.tsx:40` and `Playlist.tsx:272`, `:299`;
- a hover-only reveal on Requests' remove button (`Requests.tsx:103`).

They are invisible but still tappable. In the iPad screenshots of the album, playlist and queue pages, no row shows its ⋯.

**The change:**
- Hide controls until hover only where hover exists: Tailwind's `pointer-fine:`, already used in `Section.tsx:58`, instead of `md:`.
- **Done when** every row on iPad Safari shows its heart and ⋯, and a desktop browser still reveals them on hover.

## 2. Taking things back — S — done 2026-11-06

**The problem:** these removals are neither confirmed nor undoable.
- **Web:**
  - remove from the queue (`Queue.tsx:210`);
  - remove from a playlist (`Playlist.tsx:187`);
  - "Remove all" songs kept on the device (`OfflineStorage.tsx:82`);
  - remove a request (`Requests.tsx:97-107`);
  - stop sharing a playlist (`Playlist.tsx:162`);
  - end a device's session (`AccountsPanel.tsx:93`);
  - make a new setup code (`AccountsPanel.tsx:172`).

  Removing a download only shows a toast, with no Undo (`DownloadButton.tsx:31-33`).
- **iOS:**
  - remove from a playlist (`PlaylistItemRow.swift:49`);
  - swipe a song out of the queue (`QueueView.swift:39`);
  - remove a download (`DownloadsView.swift:82`, `:90`);
  - turn an album's download off (`DownloadButton.swift:121-125`).
- **The model to follow:** only clearing the queue offers Undo, on both apps (web `Queue.tsx:110-121`, iOS `QueueModel.swift:59-72`).

**The rule:**
- Taking something out of a list happens at once, with an Undo toast, as the queue's Clear does.
- Losing something for good gets a confirmation that names what goes: deleting a playlist, removing an account, signing out everywhere, removing all downloads.
- The toast and the words are the same in both apps (plan 5 §7).
- **Done when** every removal above has its Undo or its confirmation.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: removing from the queue, a playlist, Requests and this device, and stopping sharing, happen at once with Undo; a request's removal waits for its Undo to pass, since the server can't take it back. Deleting a playlist, removing all downloads, signing out a device, a new setup code, removing an account or a source, and clearing listening history ask first (`ConfirmDialog`). Un-hearting has no Undo yet: hearting again resets when it was favorited.*
- *iOS done 2026-11-06: removing a download and Stop sharing have Undo too, a request's removal waits for its Undo, and the same losses ask first (a popover from the control that asked).*

## 3. One song menu, everywhere — S — done 2026-11-06

**The problem:** a song's ⋯ menu changes with where you open it.
- **Web:**
  - track rows have Add to playlist and Start radio;
  - playlist rows have neither, but have Remove (`Playlist.tsx:302-311`);
  - queue rows have no Show metadata (`Queue.tsx:203-211`).
- **iOS:**
  - the queue's menu has no Start radio (`QueueView.swift:159-170`);
  - an album card's long-press has no Add to playlist (`AlbumCard.swift:29-35`).

**The changes:**
- One menu for a song, in one order: Play next · Add to queue · Add to playlist… · Start radio · Go to album · Go to artist · Show metadata. The place's own actions follow after a divider (Remove from playlist, Remove from queue, Move up, Move down). Likewise one menu for an album and one for a playlist.
- On the web, long-press on touch and right-click with a mouse open that same menu, as long-press does on iOS. (This is already in open items.)
- Add to playlist ticks the playlists that already hold the song, and can add to several at once.
  - *Done 2026-11-06 (server): `POST /api/playlists/holding` says how many of the songs each of the account's own playlists holds.*
- Play next and Add to queue confirm with a toast naming the song ("Teardrop plays next"), with Undo.
- **Done when** the same song shows the same menu in every list in both apps.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: one song menu in every list (components/menus.ts), the place's own actions after a line; right-click, a long press and the menu key open it; Add to playlist ticks the playlists holding the songs and adds to several; Play next and Add to queue toast with Undo.*
- *iOS done 2026-11-06: one song menu (`SongMenu`) from ⋯ and a long press in every list, the place's own actions after a divider; albums and playlists have one menu each (`PlayableMenu`), cards starting with Play and Shuffle; Add to playlist ticks the playlists holding the songs (`POST /api/playlists/holding`) and adds to several; Play next and Add to queue toast with Undo, as do removals from the queue and a playlist.*

## 4. Pages lead with what you came for — S–M — done 2026-11-06

- **Playlist header:** on a phone, everything comes before Play, which ends up below the fold on both apps. In order:
  - cover, kicker, title, description, meta;
  - "Shared with everyone on this server";
  - the cover's explanation ("Each style is a slime-mould species in its own colour…");
  - a line of tempo, genres, years and energy;
  - "New cover".

  Move the explanation and the numbers into the cover picker as "About this cover". Keep New cover in ⋯ and on the cover itself.
- **Album meta (iOS):** the line wraps around the format badge ("3 tracks · 17 min · 2 missing · added 4 days ago"). Use one short line (year · songs · length) with the badge after it. Plan 4 §6 already handles "2 missing".
- **Names lose to numbers when space is short:**
  - Stats truncates artists ("Massive A…", "Fleetwoo…") to fit "10 h 10 min · 105 plays", even at iPad width.
  - The iPhone queue truncates titles ("Inertia Cre…") to make room for the duration, the heart and ⋯.
  - iOS Downloads cuts off its own status ("0 of 3 so…").

  On narrow screens the numbers go on the second line, and queue rows on phones drop the heart.
- **Stats headline:** big numbers in DM Mono read as "1 , 792", because the comma takes a full monospace cell. Set the headline in the display font, or narrow the separator.
- **Artist page without a photo:** on the web, the gray blur fills half the iPad screen before the name appears. This is plan 4 §6's item.
- **Done when**, on a phone, Play shows on first view of every page you can play, and no name is cut off while numbers beside it aren't.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: the playlist's cover story and numbers moved into the cover picker's "About this cover"; covers on phones are at most 256 wide, so Play shows on the first screen of album, playlist, mix and Favorites; `NameAndNumbers` puts the numbers under the name when both don't fit (Stats, the queue, Downloads); the queue's rows on phones drop the heart; Stats' big numbers are in Gloock; an artist without a photo gets a compact round header.*
- *iOS done 2026-11-06: "About this cover" moved into the cover picker; the album's meta is one line (year · songs · length, then the badge), or the artist line then that line where it doesn't fit, never wrapping around the badge; Play shows on the first screen of every page you can play, down to the iPhone SE; `NameAndNumbers` in Stats, the queue and Downloads, and queue rows on phones drop the heart; Stats' big numbers are in Gloock; an artist without a photo of their own gets a compact round header.*

## 5. The player when it matters — M — web done 2026-11-06, iOS under way

- **Idle player:** the iPad app and the desktop web show a full player bar reading "NOTHING PLAYING", with every control disabled. Offer what to play instead: Resume (the last session), or Feeling lucky.
- **iPad keyboard (iOS):** the player bar rides above the keyboard and covers the search results, leaving two rows visible. While typing, the bar should shrink to the mini player or hide.
- **iPhone Home:** while music plays, the listening button beside Feeling lucky repeats the mini player just below it, so there are two pause buttons on one screen. While the mini player shows, the button can say what's next ("Up next: Teardrop") or step aside.
- **Now Playing in iPhone Safari:** the sheet is `fixed inset-0` (`NowPlaying.tsx:74`), and Safari 26's bars are translucent. So the page stays visible under the status bar and behind Safari's toolbar. Extend the sheet under both (large-viewport height and safe-area padding), and check it with `pnpm sim`.
- **Now Playing on the iPhone:** there's no AirPlay (plan 2 §4), no Up next (plan 4 §6) and no volume, and the space under the transport is empty. Put one row of AirPlay, Lyrics and Up next there, where Apple Music keeps them.
- **Queue on desktop:** the web player bar's Queue button opens the Queue page (`PlayerBar.tsx:56-62`), while the iPad app opens a Queue and Lyrics column beside any page. Bring that column to the web at desktop widths.
- **Done when** neither app shows a dead player, and Now Playing has the same controls on the iPhone, the web on a phone and the iPad.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: the idle bar shows the album heard last with Resume, and Feeling lucky; from 1280 px Queue and Lyrics open a column beside any page (`SideColumn`), and pages lay out by their own width; in iPhone Safari the film grain moved under the bars, so Now Playing and the tab bar color Safari's bars. iOS to do: the idle iPad bar, the iPad keyboard, iPhone Home's second pause button, iPhone Now Playing's row.*

## 6. Settings that read like a list on a phone — S–M — done 2026-11-06

- **Tabs that overflow:** the web's nine Settings tabs sit in one sideways strip. On a phone four fit and the rest are off-screen with no hint; even at iPad width, Updates is cut off. iOS shows a grouped list instead (This device · Server · Your account). On phones the web should show that same list; on wide screens keep the tabs, with a hint when they overflow.
- **"Your listening" is a destination, not a setting.** It's a Settings tab (`Settings.tsx:18`) and an iOS Settings row, and also a Library row. Keep it in Library and the account menu. ListenBrainz and "Count what this device plays" stay in Settings.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** are listed in Playback on phones and iPads that have no keyboard. Show them only where there is one, and add the `?` overlay (plan 7 §5).
- **Sources on a phone:**
  - the card squeezes its counts into a narrow column, so "On complete albums" breaks word by word;
  - it truncates the folder's path and the sync time;
  - its five icon-only buttons include two near-identical circular arrows.

  Stack the card: name, path, then the counts on one wrapping line. Put the actions in a ⋯ menu with words: Sync now, Re-read tags, Organize, Edit, Remove.
- **Accounts:**
  - the actions are icons with no words (pencil, phone, key, bin);
  - a pending invite (Jo, never redeemed) looks like any account with no plays.

  Show "Invited · not set up yet", with Copy invite link and New code. (Plan 4 §2 makes that link open the app.)
- **Done when** every Settings panel works one-handed on a phone, without sideways scrolling.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: on a phone Settings is a grouped list (This device · Your account · Server); where the page has room, the same groups are a column beside the open panel, so every panel is in view (it was a strip of tabs that scrolled sideways); Your listening lives at /stats, reached from Library and the account menu; shortcuts show only where there's a keyboard, and `?` opens them as a sleeve; Sources' cards stack, with their actions in a worded ⋯ menu; an invited account says "Invited · not set up yet", with Copy invite link and New code.*
- *iOS done 2026-11-06: Settings in the same groups (members get Your account, then Server panels read-only, saying only an admin can change them); Your listening from Library and the account menu; a source's actions in a worded ⋯ menu (Sync now, Match again, Organize files, Edit, Move up and down, Remove), each source one VoiceOver element; an invited account with Copy invite link and New code; shortcuts listed only on an iPad with a keyboard attached. Accent text in Settings is lifted to read on its lighter rows (`theme.listAccent`).*

## 7. Search never dead-ends — S–M — done 2026-11-06

- **Placeholder:** on a phone, "Artists, albums, tracks" is cut off ("…track") at the field's display size. Use a shorter phrase ("Search your music") or a smaller size.
- **Recent searches:** the empty page offers artists to try, which is good, but not what you searched for last. Add recent searches.
- **Dead end:** "Not in your crates." is the end of the road while requests are off, even though artist pages show "Albums not in your library" from MusicBrainz regardless.
  - Search should show the same results beyond the library, marked as not in your library.
    - *Done 2026-11-06 (server): without Lidarr, search asks MusicBrainz for the artists and albums (`Discover.searchMusicBrainz`). The apps still hide the section without Lidarr.*
  - Each gets Request when you may request, or "Ask {owner}" otherwise (plan 4 §4).
  - Add near matches for typos.
    - *Done 2026-11-06 (server): when nothing matches as typed, `/api/search` answers what's a letter or two away ("radiohed", "massive atack", "daftpunk"), with `near: true` for the apps to say so.*
- **Server, "Unknown artist":** every album in an artist's "Albums not in your library" says "Unknown artist". The discography request doesn't ask MusicBrainz for artist credits (`discover/musicbrainz.ts:157`), so `creditName` falls back (`:215`). On the artist's own page that line should show the year and type anyway.
  - *Done 2026-11-06 (server): the request asks for credits (`inc=artist-credits`), and without Lidarr the page takes the artist's name from the credit with its own MBID rather than the first group's, which may be a collaboration. On both apps the artist page's cards say "Album · 2022" and drop the badge.*
- **Server, bootlegs:** the same list includes bootlegs, such as "Radiotick Tracks 2" under Radiohead. Keep only release groups that have an official release.
  - *Done 2026-11-06: `release-group-status=website-default`, the filter MusicBrainz's own pages use (Radiohead: 41 groups instead of 100, without "Radiotick Tracks 2").*
- **Done when** a search for something you don't have always ends with something to do.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: recent searches on the empty page; "Not in your library" whenever there are results beyond it, with or without Lidarr; a line when results are near matches; and what to try when there's nothing anywhere. "Ask {owner}" waits for plan 4 §4.*
- *iOS done 2026-11-06: recent searches, kept per account on the device, counted on Return, a result opened or played, or words left alone for 2.5 s; "Not in your library" with or without Lidarr; on the iPhone Duo the field shows under the title. The artist page's "Not in your library" still needs Lidarr on iOS (the web shows it for any artist with a MusicBrainz ID).*

## 8. Keyboard and screen reader — M — done 2026-11-06

- **Song rows:** web song rows are `li`s with `onClick` and no focus (`TrackList.tsx:82-89`; also `Discover.tsx:213`, `Playlist.tsx:253`, `Queue.tsx:169`), so a keyboard can't play a song. Make the row a button; Enter plays.
- **Reordering** is drag-only on both apps. The web's drag handles are `span role="button"` without `tabIndex` (`Playlist.tsx:266`, `Queue.tsx:178`). Add Move up and Move down to the row's menu, and as VoiceOver actions on iOS.
- **Web mini player:** it's a `div role="button"` that contains buttons (`PlayerBar.tsx:109-130`). Split it into an "Open now playing" button plus the controls.
- **Renaming a playlist** is a tap on its title, and nothing more (web `Playlist.tsx:100-108`, iOS `PlaylistView.swift:110`). Give both apps a visible Rename in ⋯, and show a pencil on hover or focus.
- **iOS toasts:** they're dismissed by a tap gesture only (`ToastsView.swift:25`); add an accessibility action. Their "Later" and action buttons are under 44 pt (`ToastsView.swift:15-16`).
- **Touch targets under 44:**
  - web: `Requests.tsx:103`, `Discover.tsx:35`, `Toasts.tsx:70`, `Dialog.tsx:80` and the seek bar's hit area (`Slider.tsx:96`);
  - iOS: small pills, the small download button, chips, "Read more", "Show all", and Queue's Edit and Clear.

  The shared controls in plan 5 §2 fix these.
  - *Done 2026-11-06: web controls take 44 px on touch screens (`tap-target`), and the seek bar's hit area is taller; on iOS pills, the download button, chips, "Read more", "Show all" and Queue's Edit and Clear take 44 points. Toasts' buttons are still small on iOS.*
- **Done when** a keyboard alone can play, queue, reorder and rename on the web, and VoiceOver can do the same on iOS.
- *Web rows done 2026-11-06: a row's title is its own button (Enter plays); drag handles are focusable buttons moved with the arrow keys, and Move up/Move down are in the row's menu; the mini player is an "Open now playing" button beside its controls; a playlist's ⋯ has Rename, and its title shows a pencil.*
- *iOS done 2026-11-06: Move up and Move down are VoiceOver actions on queue and playlist rows, and in their menus; Rename is in a playlist's ⋯ and a VoiceOver action on its title; toasts have a Dismiss action and answer the escape gesture, their Undo held while VoiceOver is on it, and their buttons take 44 points.*

## 9. Signals that mean one thing — S — done 2026-11-06

- **The "on this device" mark:** as the next songs load ahead, they get the same mark as a download the listener asked for (`OfflineMark.tsx:35`, "On this device"). An album that's playing then shows songs marked as kept that nobody downloaded. Keep the mark for downloads; songs cached ahead get nothing, or a fainter variant.
- **Requests without Lidarr:** the Requests page still says "Albums you asked Lidarr to find" and "Search for something… and hit Request" on a server with no Lidarr, where no Request button exists. Say what's possible instead: "Requests need a Lidarr source", with a link for admins and a line for everyone else.
- **Done when** every badge and mark means one thing in both apps.
- *Web done 2026-11-06: `OfflineMark` marks only downloaded songs (`useIsDownloaded`), and Requests without Lidarr, with requests off, or for an account that can't request says why, with Add Lidarr or Turn them on for admins.*
- *iOS done 2026-11-06: the mark only for downloads (`DownloadStore.isDownloaded`), and the same Requests lines per role (`NoRequests`).*
