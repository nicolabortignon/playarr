# Plan 11: iOS widgets

Agreed with the owner on 2026-11-20. This reverses the decision of 2026-11-04 to have no widgets ([decisions.md](../decisions.md)), and takes up the widget and Control Center ideas the [delight plan](2026-11-05-7-delight.md) parked under "Bigger ideas".

**Goal:** Playarr on the Home Screen. Separate widgets each do one thing: start the music, go back to an album, play a mix, follow what's playing. Each comes in the sizes that suit it, and a Feeling lucky control goes in Control Center, on the Lock Screen and on the Action button.

## The widgets

| Widget | Sizes | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Quick play | small, medium | Feeling lucky, Play my Favorites and Start radio as buttons. Small has Feeling lucky only. |
| Jump back in | medium, large | 4 or 8 of the albums heard lately (Home's Jump back in, `API.Library.recentAlbums`). A tap plays the album. |
| Made for you | medium, large | 4 or 8 of Home's mixes, with the covers the app draws for them. A tap plays the mix. |
| Now Playing | small, medium | Cover, song and artist, and a progress bar that runs on its own (`ProgressView(timerInterval:)`). Medium adds previous, play/pause and next. |
| Feeling lucky (control) | Control Center, Lock Screen, Action button | `ControlWidgetButton` running Feeling lucky |

- **No snapshot yet, or signed out:** each widget says "Open Playarr to sign in" and opens the app when tapped.
- **Look:** the widgets take the cover's accent, which the app writes into the snapshot, and stay readable in the tinted and clear Home Screen styles.

## How it fits together

- **A widget extension, `PlayarrWidgets`** (`$(PLAYARR_BUNDLE_ID).widgets`), in `project.yml`, embedded in the app.
- **A fourth package, `PlayarrWidgetData`**, with no dependencies. It holds the snapshot's model and reads and writes it in the App Group's folder. The app (through `PlayarrUI`) and the extension both use it. The extension doesn't link `PlayarrUI`, to stay well inside a widget's memory.
- **The intents are compiled into both the app and the extension** (`Playarr/Intents`).
  - In the extension they're declarations only, under the `WIDGET_EXTENSION` condition. Audio playback intents are performed by the system in the app's process, so a widget's tap plays without opening Playarr.
  - In the app they keep calling `QuickPlay` through `AppModel`.
  - Phase 1 proves on an iPhone that the tap plays with the app closed.
- **An App Group, `group.me.tracklistme.playarr`**, is the app's first entitlement and the extension's. It's how the app hands the widgets their data.
- **The widgets never contact a server.** The app writes `widget/snapshot.json` in the group's folder, and covers as small JPEGs (about 300 px) beside it. The snapshot holds the active account, up to 8 albums heard lately, up to 8 mixes, and what's playing. After writing, the app calls `WidgetCenter.reloadTimelines`.
- **When it's written:**
  - as Home's albums heard lately and the mixes load;
  - as the player changes song or plays and pauses;
  - again on switching accounts.
  - Signing out clears it, and the demo never writes one.
- **Reloads:** iOS doesn't count reloads against a widget's budget while the app is in front or playing audio, so Now Playing can follow each song.
- **A tap that fails** (offline, or the session ended) shows nothing on the Home Screen. The app logs why, as it does for Siri.

## Phases

1. **The extension, the package and the App Group.** The intents compiled into both, Quick play, the Feeling lucky control, and a snapshot holding only who's signed in. Check on a device that a tap plays with the app closed.
2. **The snapshot's albums and mixes, with covers.** Jump back in and Made for you, with `PlayAlbumIntent` and `PlayMixIntent`.
3. **Now Playing.** What's playing in the snapshot, reloads as it changes, and the play/pause, next and previous intents.

## Tests and checks

- **Unit tests:** the snapshot's reading and writing, the app's snapshot writer (switching accounts, signing out, the demo), the new intents' actions (`QuickPlay`), and the timeline provider turning a snapshot into entries.
- **By hand on a device:** adding each widget, tapping each button with the app closed, the control in Control Center and on the Action button, the tinted and clear styles, and an iPad.
- **Signing:** the archive is unsigned and the export signs it automatically with the App Store Connect key, which has to register the extension's bundle id and the App Group. `release.mjs` checks the exported app and extension carry the App Group, and stops if they don't.
